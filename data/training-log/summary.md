**Coverage:** 2024-08-26 to 2025-10-01. That's 245 logged sessions, 44 planned sessions with no notes, and 433 extracted results (205 lifts, 142 metcons, 39 benchmarks, 26 erg pieces, 21 gymnastics tests). The sheet also has planned sessions through 2025-11-14, but nothing was logged after 2025-10-03 ("Surgery Day"). Shoulder pain shows up from May 2025 onward and limits jerks, SHSPU and overhead work.
